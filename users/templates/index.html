Core Capabilities

Everything Your Coaching Staff Needs

Player Detection

YOLOv8 localises every player, referee and the ball with precise bounding boxes and team color classification at each frame.

Multi-Object Tracking

ByteTrack assigns stable persistent IDs across all frames — surviving occlusions, tackles, and high-density crowd situations.

Speed & Distance

Homography pitch mapping converts pixel coordinates to real meters — delivering per-player speed curves and total distance covered.

Possession Analytics

Frame-level ball proximity generates live possession %, pressure maps, and zone-control heatmaps for tactical review.
